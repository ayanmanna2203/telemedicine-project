FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Is SwasthConnect really free?

Yes. Every consultation booked through SwasthConnect is free for citizens, funded as a public health service under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare.

Do I need an ABHA health ID to register?

You'll need your mobile number linked to your Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) ID to register and to keep your consultation history in one place.

What if I don't have a smartphone?

You can join most consultations by a simple phone call — no app or smartphone required. Ask your local Primary Health Centre for assisted booking if needed.

Are the doctors verified?

Every doctor on SwasthConnect is checked against the National Medical Register before being empanelled to consult on the platform.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

SwasthConnect is not for emergencies. Please call 108 immediately or go to your nearest hospital.
