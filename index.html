The process

Talk to a doctor in three steps

No app download required — e-Kabiraj runs in your phone's browser, even on a basic 3G connection.

Register with your ABHA ID

Sign up with your mobile number and Ayushman Bharat Health Account — no paperwork, verified in under a minute.

Pick a doctor & a slot

Choose a specialty, see live availability, and book the next open slot — often within the hour.

Consult by video or phone

Join the call from any phone. Get an e-prescription instantly and collect medicine from any government pharmacy.

412 doctors online right now

Average wait time today: 8 minutes across general medicine.
