Healthcare that reaches every last mile

SwasthConnect is the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare's National Telemedicine Service, built to put a qualified doctor within reach of every citizen — regardless of geography or income.

Our mission

Bridging the gap between citizens and care

Launched under Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, SwasthConnect connects patients in remote and underserved areas with a nationwide network of empanelled doctors — by video, phone, or chat, in the language they're most comfortable with.

Every consultation is free, every doctor is verified against the National Medical Register, and every record is tied securely to your ABHA health ID under the DPDP Act, 2023.

What we stand for

Our values

Free, always

No consultation fee, no subscription — funded entirely as a public health service.

Private by design

Health records are encrypted and processed under the DPDP Act, 2023 — never sold or shared.

Verified doctors only

Every doctor is checked against the National Medical Register before being empanelled.
